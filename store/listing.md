Tap to fly. Grab batteries. Don't run dry.

Car Rocket is a fast one-tap arcade game. Pilot a red electric roadster through a city of trees, buildings, cranes and birds. Your aero charge drains while you fly, so grab batteries to stay in the air. When the charge hits zero, the car starts to fall.

FEATURES
- Simple one-tap controls, hard to master
- Draw a small circle on screen to flip your car upside down and back
- Unlock new car skins by playing and scoring points
- Reach 100 points in one game after unlocking every car to earn the shining Platinum gold car
- Share your score and your new cars with friends on Instagram, Facebook, TikTok and X
- Short rounds, perfect for a quick break

Can you beat your best score?

Car Rocket: Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Car Rocket is a casual arcade game. This policy explains what information the game and its third party services handle.

What we collect

We do not ask you to create an account and we do not collect your name, email or location. Your best score, play count and chosen car are stored only on your device.

Advertising

The app shows ads provided by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use device information such as your advertising ID, IP address and general device data to show and measure ads and to prevent fraud. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in your device settings. See Google's privacy policy at policies.google.com/privacy.

Purchases

If you buy the optional "Remove ads" item, the payment is handled by Google Play or the Apple App Store. We receive confirmation that the purchase was made through RevenueCat, but never your payment details.

Sharing your result

The share buttons create an image of your result on your device and open your phone's share menu. Nothing is sent anywhere unless you choose an app and send it yourself.

Children

Car Rocket is not directed to children under 13.
